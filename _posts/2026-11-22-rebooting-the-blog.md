My last post here dates back to February 2020. Almost seven years later, I am finally rebooting this blog.

<!-- more -->

I will continue writing about Symfony and the web development ecosystem. But I will also be documenting my AI journey: how it changes the way I work, what works for me, and what doesn't.

Nowadays, long and verbose articles are cheap to produce. I came to realize brevity is what matters. So I will try to keep posts short and focused, and I hope it will leave more room for discussion in the comments.
